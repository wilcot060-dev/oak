Our Evolution

Historical Milestones

2016

Humble Beginnings

Oak Furniture Interiors started as a private atelier designing custom wardrobes for select historic residences.

2019

Workshop Extension

Expanded operations to local assembly centers, employing master carvers and establishing high-fidelity quality assurance.

2022

Oak Furniture and Interiors Alliance

Merged and consolidated with Oak Furniture and Interiors properties, providing integrated design consulting alongside material sales.

2026

Digital Transition

Reaching modern clients with virtual spatial showrooms and fully streamlined remote consultation services.
