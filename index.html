Privacy Policy

Before you share personal details with us, please read how we handle them. Please do not share payment card numbers, passwords, ID numbers or other highly sensitive information through this website or the chat assistant.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Oak Furniture & Interiors ("Oak", "we", "us"), a furniture and interior design business. We are the party responsible for the personal information described below.

2. Information we collect

  • Details you give us: name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, preferred session date, and project notes (such as room size, materials or style preferences) when you use the booking form, the chat assistant, the newsletter sign-up or WhatsApp links.
  • Your preferences on this device: display choices (accent colour, room, compact cards, reduced motion), chat history for the current visit, and your acknowledgement of this policy.
  • Technical data: your IP address and browser details are visible to the third-party services listed in section 5 when their content loads.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, arrange consultations and quote for furniture or design work.
  • To send style recommendations and catalogue news, only if you subscribed to the newsletter.
  • To operate, secure and improve the website.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Your consent

We process the details you submit because you choose to provide them and ask us to respond. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us (section 10); this does not affect what was done before withdrawal.

5. Sharing and third parties

  • WhatsApp (Meta): booking and enquiry forms open WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. Once sent, it is handled under WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.
  • Content providers: fonts, icons, images and video are loaded from third-party services (for example Google Fonts, cdnjs, Unsplash and image hosts), which may receive your IP address and browser information.
  • Social links: Instagram, Facebook and Pinterest are separate services with their own policies.
  • We may also disclose information where required by law.

6. Storage on your device

We use your browser's local and session storage for the preferences, chat history and policy acknowledgement described above. Nothing sensitive is stored there, and you can clear it at any time through your browser settings or the "Reset" option in the menu.

7. Retention

We keep enquiry and booking details only as long as needed to respond to you, deliver the work you request and meet legal or accounting obligations, then delete or anonymise them.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please share only what is necessary.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to its use, and to withdraw consent or unsubscribe at any time.

10. Children

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available from the "Privacy Policy" link on this website. Last updated: 8 November 2026.

12. Contact us

For any privacy request or question, message us on WhatsApp at +255 655 761 313.
